Tie-breakers

  • E1 Fighting the bureau / fixing the report → Credit reporting. Lender mishandled the account → the product.
  • E2 Fighting the collector → Debt collection.
  • E3 Original bank chasing its own debt → the product.
  • E4 Debit card → Bank account. Zelle / wire / PayPal → Money transfer.
  • E5 Student & car loans → Consumer loan.
  • E6 Two products → the one whose team must act.
  • E8 Scams → the channel the money moved through.
  • E7 None → no financial product at all (rare).
